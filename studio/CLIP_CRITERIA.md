# CLIP SELECTION CRITERIA — Type 2 (locked, user-driven)

> Source: user feedback 2026-10-24. Ye rules har Type 2 build par lagenge.

## Core rule
**Clip = EVENT, clip ≠ ambience.**

**Test:** clip ka 2 second dekho aur poochho — *"isme hua kya?"*
- Jawab = "car chal rahi hai / road hai / lights hain" → **REJECT**
- Jawab = "accident hone-hone bacha / banda gira / tyre slip hui" → **SELECT**

## Scoring (5 me se jitne pass)
1. **Event clear hai?** near-miss / crash / fall / dumb decision — nahi to entry nahi.
2. **Payoff frame ke andar dikhta hai?** (sirf sound ya baad me pata chalta ho → reject)
3. **Label ek line me moment bata deta hai?** e.g. "the near miss", "the wipeout", "the guy who thought he could"
4. **Tone** = tension + halki comedy. Tragedy / injury-porn nahi.
5. **"Iska ye karne ki zaroorat kya thi?"** — dumb decision visible ho.

## Kya achha hai
- Car: sudden brake, near-miss, kiss-close collision, tyre slip, banda bhaag ke bacha
- Sports: trick fail, board toot gaya, balance gaya
- General: slip, trip, object break, dog jumps in, plan ulta pad gaya

## Kya reject hai
- Night drive / highway / car lights / empty road — kuch nahi hua
- Sirf camera movement (pan/tilt/drone) — ambience
- Slow-mo me kuch na ho raha ho
- Staged actor clip jo asli fail jaisa na lage (last resort only)

## Chhoot
- **Opening establishing shot allowed** — max **1.0–1.5 s**, 6.6 s ka slot nahi.

## Sources
- Free/clean: Pixabay, Vecteezy, Mixkit, Pexels (fails yahan staged hote hain, par safe)
- User-given viral links: try karo; YouTube download IP-blocked hai, Instagram login-wall — fail ho to user ko batao, chup-chaap skip nahi
